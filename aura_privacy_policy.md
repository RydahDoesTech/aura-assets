## Aura Privacy Policy

Rydah provides Aura, an Android home screen widget and wallpaper app. This policy describes the information Aura accesses, how it is used and shared, and the choices available to you. Contact: apps@thisizrydah.com.

Aura does not require you to create an Aura account. Aura stores settings, widget setups, favorites and cached content on your device. Some features also connect to the services described below. Those services receive network information such as your IP address and request details and handle it under their own policies.

**Information and permissions**

You can use different features without granting every permission. Android lets you manage permissions in its settings.

* **Location:** If you choose current-location weather, Aura uses the approximate or precise location you allow. It sends coordinates rounded to three decimal places to your selected weather provider, The Weather Channel or Open-Meteo. Rounded coordinates can still identify an area and are not anonymous. Android's geocoder may use a platform-provided service to look up a city name or a city you search for. You can instead select a city manually.
* **Background location:** If you grant this separately, Aura may update weather for your current location while the app is closed. Without it, Aura can use a previously obtained location or a manually selected city, subject to Android's background limits.
* **Calendar:** With your permission, calendar widgets read event information on your device to display it. Aura's weather and online-content requests do not include calendar events.
* **Usage access:** If you grant access in Android settings, the Storage widget uses app usage/storage information on your device to show storage usage.
* **Media and notification access:** If you grant notification-listener access, Android grants the app access through its notification-listener interface. Aura uses media-session information to display and control playback; its listener does not read notification text to infer playback. Media information remains on your device except when you choose to share content with another app.
* **Notifications:** Aura asks whether you want optional Aura updates and wallpaper alerts. Android notification permission and the OneSignal subscription are separate controls, described below.
* **Wallpaper and device operation:** Aura uses Android's wallpaper capability when you choose to apply an image. Android also allows Aura to receive startup events and offer battery-optimization settings so widgets can update.

**Online services**

* **The Weather Channel or Open-Meteo:** Your chosen provider receives the rounded coordinates needed to return weather, along with ordinary network and request information. See [The Weather Channel privacy policy](https://weather.com/en-US/twc/privacy-policy) and [Open-Meteo terms and privacy information](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms).
* **GitHub and wallpaper hosts:** Aura downloads the wallpaper catalog, preview images and hosted quotes from a public GitHub content repository. Full-size wallpaper images may come from the hosts named in the catalog, including Unsplash, Pixabay, Pexels, Google Drive and Imgur. The host you contact can receive your IP address and request information. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
* **Wikipedia:** Today in History retrieves events from Wikipedia; events are cached on your device. See the [Wikimedia privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy). Did You Know and Word of the Day use bundled content; opening a linked article or entry takes you to that external service.
* **Cloudflare:** When you run Speed Test, Aura transfers test data to and from Cloudflare to measure your connection. Your IP address and request information reach Cloudflare; results are kept on your device. The test can use substantial mobile data. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
* **OneSignal and Google:** Aura uses OneSignal for optional push notifications when its notification service is configured. Aura requires your affirmative choice before enabling OneSignal consent. OneSignal and the platform push service may process a push token, subscription identifiers, IP address, notification permission and delivery information, app and device details, language, time zone, and session or notification-engagement information to provide and measure notifications. OneSignal may infer a general location from your IP address. Aura does not enable OneSignal GPS-location sharing. See [OneSignal's privacy policy](https://onesignal.com/privacy_policy) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Turning off notifications in Android stops notification display or delivery according to Android's settings; it does not by itself guarantee that OneSignal consent is withdrawn or that records are deleted. Contact Rydah if you want help requesting deletion of a OneSignal record.

When you share a quote or open a link, your chosen app or website receives the content or request you choose to send, under its own privacy practices.

**Storage, backups and deletion**

Aura keeps settings, favorites and cached data locally. Aura does not operate its own cloud backup service or automatically upload its exported settings backup to a cloud provider. Its Back up settings feature writes a file to the location you choose and reads a file you select for restore; if you choose a cloud-backed location or upload the file yourself, that service handles the copy. Separately, Android device backup or transfer may retain eligible app data according to your device and backup settings.

Uninstalling Aura removes its ordinary app-local data from the device. It does not necessarily delete exported backup files, downloaded wallpaper images, device/cloud backups, data already held by service providers, or copies you shared. You can delete exported files and downloaded wallpapers where you saved them and manage device backups using your device or backup service. To request help with OneSignal data, contact apps@thisizrydah.com; Rydah will assess and process requests as applicable, but cannot guarantee how quickly another provider acts or deletes its records.

If you contact Rydah, your message and contact details are used to respond and handle the request and are retained only as long as reasonably needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. Service providers apply their own retention practices.

**Security**

Aura uses HTTPS for its configured online services. Wallpaper links in a catalog can point to third-party hosts, and the app accepts both HTTP and HTTPS image URLs. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

**Children's privacy**

Aura is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Aura or its service providers, contact apps@thisizrydah.com so Rydah can review the request.

**Changes and contact**

Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the app or through another appropriate notice; where required, consent will be obtained before new processing. Questions or privacy requests: apps@thisizrydah.com.
